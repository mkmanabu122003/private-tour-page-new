How It Works · From Inquiry to Tour Day

Four steps, four moments.

Each step has a clear timing and a clear "no-pressure" rule. You can stop or adjust at any point before payment.

Quick answer

You're never locked in until Step 03.

Steps 01 and 02 are conversation. Step 03 is when you decide. Step 04 is the tour itself.

01 2 min

Send Your Request

Tell me your dates, group size, and what excites you about Tokyo. Free to ask anything — no commitment.

02 Within 24h

Plan Together

I reply with a personalized itinerary and a flat quote. Adjust the plan as many times as you want — vegetarian-friendly stops, slower pace, kid-friendly route, anything.

03 No rush

Approve & Pay

Pay only when the plan is exactly right. Secure card payment, simple receipt, free cancellation up to 7 days before.

04 4–8 hrs

Walk Tokyo Together

Meet at your hotel or station. I adapt the day in real time — slower if you're tired, deeper if you're curious, lunch wherever the plan takes us.
