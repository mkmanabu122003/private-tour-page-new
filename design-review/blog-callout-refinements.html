Callout Redesign

Less boxed, more guided.

Replace generic note boxes with modules that feel like Manabu's judgment: guide notes, field notes, decision cards, route strips, and editorial pull quotes.

Current Pattern

avoid
Replace broad tables with compact modules.

Tables look AI-generated when every row is equally weighted. Use them only for exact data. For judgment calls, cards feel more human and easier to scan.

Why it feels weak: generic blog-template treatment, no voice, no visual hierarchy beyond the left rule.
